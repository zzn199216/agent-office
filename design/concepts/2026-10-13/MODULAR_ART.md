# Selected office art implemented as modules

Reference: `06-minimal-editorial-office.png`, selected by the user on 2026-10-13.

The reference was supplied to the built-in imagegen tool for four independent transparent-background assets. The artwork is stored in `web/assets/office/`; the original generated files remain in the imagegen output directory. SHA256, dimensions and byte counts are recorded in the public `AVATAR_SOURCES.json` office_art section.

Shared direction: warm minimalist editorial cartoon, thin organic charcoal contour, pale ash wood, satin silver equipment, soft sage seating, mild high-angle front view, screen-aligned forms, subtle contact shading, readable silhouette at small sizes. No full room, baked floor, interface labels, company logos or watermark. Actual transparent alpha background, complete uncropped objects with clear margins.

| Module | Requested subject |

| furniture-desk.png | One furniture-only pale-wood desk with thin silver legs, a silver monitor back and compact keyboard. No chair, character or accessories. |

The scene composes modules per real member. The floor and architectural background remain independently configurable and can grow downward. Names, provider identity badges, status indicators, clock and message bubbles remain live DOM elements. The AI modules do not create membership, online status or messages.
