## Refinement of direction 01

The user selected the warm illustration and requested fewer visual details, a subtle geek character, and the restraint of Apple product design. Two further images were generated with direction 01 as an explicit image reference, using the built-in imagegen tool. The originals remain untouched.

- 04-warm-minimal-geek-office.png — warm minimal hand-drawn studio: fewer props, graphite monitors, unified furniture, open floor and a few tidy cables.
- 05-minimal-silver-office.png — more restrained alternative: silver monitors, compact light keyboards, fewer accessories, no entrance rug, and one small docking hub.

Both prompts retain the upper-left entrance, left aisle, three columns, eight seated coworkers and one newcomer. They ask for warm-white architecture, very light wood, muted sage seating, coherent rounded devices, deliberate contour lines, soft contact shadows, small separate identity badges and only two empty speech bubbles. They remove side cabinets, book piles, wall art, floor pets, repeated desk plants, busy material textures and decorative clutter. The silver variant further removes rugs and reduces device colors.

The requested geek feeling comes from equipment precision, a small robot and restrained technical details. The prompts explicitly exclude cyberpunk, RGB lights, Matrix text, photoreal showrooms, Apple logos and rendered interface frames. The resulting window scenery and badge colors remain busier than requested; these are candidates for further simplification when extracting reusable art. These images are visual studies only, not installed application artwork.
